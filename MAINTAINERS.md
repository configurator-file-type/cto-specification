# Maintainers, Editors and Working Group Leads

Roles are defined in [`Governance.md`](Governance.md) Section 1 and Section 6.2:

- **Maintainer:** runs the Working Group's process. Organizes its activities,
  determines consensus, handles appeals, and merges approved pull requests. Each
  Working Group has at least two, from different Members.
- **Editor:** looks after the specification text. Makes sure it reflects what the
  Working Group decided and follows the formatting and content guidelines, and
  gives final approval on specification changes.
- **Working Group Lead:** one of the Maintainers. Convenes meetings, sets
  agendas, and is the Working Group's point of contact. No extra decision-making
  authority.

One person may hold more than one role, and may hold roles in both Working
Groups. Changes to this file follow Governance.md 6.2. Role holders for the
CIS Specification Working Group are listed in
<https://github.com/configurator-file-type/cis-specification/blob/main/MAINTAINERS.md>.
