## Antitrust

The Project operates under the JDF Antitrust Policy:
<https://jointdevelopment.org/policies/antitrust-policy/>. In every Project
space, do not discuss prices, costs, margins, bids, customers, market allocation
or any company's unannounced product plans. The file formats have fields for
price, cost and lead time. Discussing the fields is fine; discussing any
company's actual figures is not. Use invented figures in example files.
